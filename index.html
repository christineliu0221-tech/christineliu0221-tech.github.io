MARKET PERSPECTIVE

What I am watching

Three shifts shaping the future of audit and risk—and why they matter for the work I want to do.

A

AI adoption needs professional judgment

PCAOB outreach found that GenAI use in audits was still concentrated in administrative and research activities, even as firms continued investing. The opportunity is real, but so are supervision, privacy, security, and reliability requirements.

Why it matters

Christine's advantage is the combination of technical curiosity and the audit judgment needed to evaluate outputs rather than accept them at face value.

Source: PCAOB staff spotlight ↗
B

Analytics must remain reviewable

PCAOB audit-documentation requirements emphasize a clear record of the evidence supporting significant conclusions and the ability to plan, supervise, and review the work.

Why it matters

That makes structured workpapers, traceable analysis, and reviewer-ready communication essential capabilities in data-enabled assurance.

Source: PCAOB AS 1215 ↗
C

Digital disruption is now a board-level risk

The IIA's Risk in Focus 2026 research placed digital disruption, including AI, second in the global risk ranking. Forty-eight percent of respondents rated it a top-five risk, up nine percentage points year over year.

Why it matters

Audit and risk teams need professionals who can connect technology change to controls, financial impact, and practical business response.

Source: IIA Risk in Focus 2026 ↗
