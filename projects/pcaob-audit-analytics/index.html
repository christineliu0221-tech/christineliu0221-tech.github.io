CASE STUDY 02 · DATA-ENABLED ASSURANCE

PCAOB Audit Analytics

A dashboard becomes valuable only when its signals are connected to financial-statement risk, business context, and a clear follow-up question.

Environment
PCAOB-compliant audit
Analytical view
Power BI outputs
Focus areas
Inventory · COGS · Revenue

CASE STRUCTURE

ContextContributionApproachOutcomeRelevance

01 · CONTEXT

The business and assurance question

The audit of a global electronics distributor involved large and interconnected financial-statement areas. Analytical outputs could surface movements at scale, but those movements still needed to be interpreted in relation to the business, account relationships, and audit risk.

02 · MY CONTRIBUTION

Clear ownership within a larger engagement

I collaborated with a data analytics support team and reviewed Power BI dashboard outputs across inventory, cost of goods sold, and revenue. My contribution was to connect the visual patterns with audit context and identify movements that warranted attention or explanation.

03 · APPROACH

A structured path from evidence to conclusion

  1. 01

    Reviewed the dashboard structure and the relationship between the three financial-statement areas.

  2. 02

    Compared trends and movements to expected account behavior and the underlying business context.

  3. 03

    Flagged unusual patterns and translated visual signals into focused follow-up questions.

  4. 04

    Connected analytical observations with the broader audit approach and documentation needs.

04 · OUTCOME

Decision value

Used data-enabled review to identify trends and unusual movements that could inform audit focus and follow-up, while keeping interpretation grounded in financial-statement relationships.

05 · MARKET RELEVANCE

Why this work transfers

The work reflects the central challenge in audit analytics: technology can expand coverage, but professionals must still decide which signals matter, why they matter, and what evidence is needed next. Christine brings the financial context required to make analytics actionable.
