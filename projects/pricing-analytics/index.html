CASE STUDY 03 · ANALYTICAL PROTOTYPING

AI-Assisted Pricing Analytics

A focused prototype exploring whether historical engagement characteristics could make professional fee estimates more consistent and evidence-based.

Tool
Python
Method
Multiple linear regression
Model inputs
8 variables

CASE STRUCTURE

ContextContributionApproachOutcomeRelevance

01 · CONTEXT

The business and assurance question

Professional service pricing often blends experience, expected effort, client characteristics, and judgment. The project explored whether historical engagement data could supplement that judgment with a repeatable analytical reference point.

02 · MY CONTRIBUTION

Clear ownership within a larger engagement

I prototyped a pricing analysis workflow in Python with AI assistance, structured eight explanatory variables, applied multiple linear regression, and evaluated model fit. The project was exploratory rather than a production pricing system.

03 · APPROACH

A structured path from evidence to conclusion

  1. 01

    Translated the business question into a defined target and a set of eight potential explanatory variables.

  2. 02

    Prepared the analytical workflow and used Python to estimate a multiple linear regression model.

  3. 03

    Reviewed model fit and considered how variable relationships could be interpreted in a pricing context.

  4. 04

    Identified where additional data, validation, and governance would be required before operational use.

04 · OUTCOME

Decision value

Demonstrated how a familiar professional-services decision could be reframed as a testable analytical question, while making the limits of a prototype explicit.

05 · MARKET RELEVANCE

Why this work transfers

Strong analytics work is not defined by the most complex model. It is defined by whether the method fits the question, the result can be explained, and decision-makers understand the limitations. This project shows business framing, technical growth, and responsible interpretation.
